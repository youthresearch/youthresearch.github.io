Before you submit

  • Review the academic integrity standards
  • Set your Drive or Dropbox sharing to "Anyone with the link," and test the link in a private/incognito window first
  • Remove personal identifying info from figures/images if required by your teacher or program
  • Have your citations ready in the required format
  • Original research needs 5+ figures/tables; reports need 4 or fewer

Timeline

Most submissions receive an initial decision within 8–14 weeks of the editorial check.
